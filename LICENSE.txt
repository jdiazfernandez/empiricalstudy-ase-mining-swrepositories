WHAT IS NOT IN THIS PACKAGE, AND WHY:

No content of the mined repositories is included -- no rule-file text, no
cloned source. Those files remain under whatever licence each repository
declares, and most declare none, which means all rights reserved. The package
instead ships content-addressed pointers (data/replication/pi2_corpus_pointers.csv)
from which any coded file can be retrieved from its source and verified by hash.
